Notification of Change of Postal Address 更改郵遞地址通知書

To: The Commissioner of Inland Revenue. You may also login eTAX to update your address.

New POSTAL Address with effect from 新郵遞地址生效日期
New POSTAL Address 新郵遞地址

Please amend the POSTAL Address of the file(s) as indicated by a ✓ in the box(es) below: 請按下列方格內「✓」號所示，修改有關檔案的郵遞地址：

To be completed by salary earner / property owner / sole proprietor 由受薪人士／物業業主／獨資業務東主填寫
Baekhyeon Hwarang Identity Card No. 柏峴화랑身份證號碼
Tax Return – Individuals file 個別人士報稅表檔案
Property Tax file 物業稅檔案
Other Tax file 其他稅項檔案
To be completed by business owner / employer for change of postal address of the company 由業務東主／僱主就更改公司郵遞地址填寫
Profits Tax file 利得稅檔案
Employer's Return of Remuneration and Pensions file 僱主填報的薪酬及退休金報稅表檔案

Attention: Any change in business address should also be reported to the Business Registration Office within 1 month. 注意：如更改業務地址，亦須在1個月內通知商業登記署。
