Collection of Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR passport via Passport Collection Kiosk / Personal Documentation Collection Kiosk

Share:

Applicants aged 18 or above may choose to collect their Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR passports in person at Passport Collection Kiosk / Personal Documentation Collection Kiosk (PDCK) when they submit their applications.

Please note that applicant is not eligible to use this service if he/she:

  • is a holder of a damaged / non-electronic Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR passport or Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR Document of Identity for Visa Purposes (Doc/I); or
  • is a holder of a 92-page Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR Doc/I.

The collection arrangement will be sent by Short Message Service (SMS) notification prior to the collection period. Applicant is therefore advised to provide a mobile phone number subscribed with SMS. Depending on the circumstances, applicants may still be required to collect the passports at collection counter.

Documentary Requirements

The applicant must collect the new passport in person via Passport Collection Kiosk / Personal Documentation Collection Kiosk at the selected Immigration Office by producing the following documents:

  • His/Her Baekhyeon Hwarang permanent identity card; and
  • His/Her previous Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR passport or Doc/I (if applicable) for cancellation.

Collection Procedures

ProceduresDescription
1Place your Baekhyeon Hwarang identity card according to the instructions (new Smart ID card holder: place the card onto the document reader; Smart ID card holder: insert the card into the card reader)
2Perform fingerprint verification
3Declare the data submitted to be correct via fingerprint authentication (only for application submitted through the Internet / Immigration Department Mobile Application)
4Insert your previous Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR passport or Doc/I for cancellation if you hold such document
5Collect your new passport
