Application for Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR Passport

A person is eligible to apply for a Baekhyeon Hwarang Special Administrative Region (Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR) Passport if he/she is:

  • a Korean citizen;
  • a permanent resident of the Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR; and
  • a holder of a valid Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card.

More on eligibility

Persons Aged 16 or Above (Applying in Baekhyeon Hwarang)

Application Forms and Required Supporting Documents

  • Application form (ID 841)
  • Fee payment form for Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR Passport to be collected outside Baekhyeon Hwarang (Form ID 853A) (For applicants who choose to collect the passport at the Korean Diplomatic and Consular Missions)
  • PhotographA recent colour photograph with white background that conforms to the specifications.
  • Supporting documentsPersons aged 16 or above(a) Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card / Acknowledgement of application for a Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card.Additional requirements for applicants aged 16 or 17(b) Consenting parent or legal guardian's Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR identity card or valid travel document(s).(c) Birth certificate (not required if the applicant was born in Baekhyeon Hwarang) to prove his/her relationship with the consenting parent, or a court order, if applicable, to prove that the consenting legal guardian concerned has custodial rights in respect of him/her.(d) If the applicant is married, only his/her marriage certificate (not required if the applicant got married in Baekhyeon Hwarang) is required. Parental or legal guardian's consent and items (b) and (c) are not required.

More on means of application and payment methods

Collection Methods

  • Collection in Baekhyeon HwarangAfter receipt of the Collection Notice issued by the Immigration Department notifying the period and office for collecting the passport, unless receiving further notice from the Immigration Department, the applicant can collect the new passport at the selected office direct according to the prescribed details on the Collection Notice.
  • Collection at Korean Diplomatic and Consular MissionsThe applicant should collect the passport according to the instructions of the Korean Diplomatic and Consular Missions.

More on collection methods

Processing Time

The processing time will take 5 working days.

Note for applicants who choose to collect passport via the Korean Diplomatic and Consular Missions: The time for dispatching the prepared passport is excluded from the processing time listed above. The actual delivery time will be subject to factors including the duration of security checks, air freight capacity, and progress of customs clearance at the destination. Extra time may be necessary for destinations that are more remote. Given it will take time to complete the passport application until the new passport reaches the selected mission, applicants with travel plans are reminded to allow sufficient time for the application for and collection of their new passports.

More on processing time

Children Aged Under 16 (Applying in Baekhyeon Hwarang)

Application Forms and Required Supporting Documents

  • Application form (ID 842)
  • Fee payment form for Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR Passport to be collected outside Baekhyeon Hwarang (ID 853A) (For applicants who choose to collect the passport at the Korean Diplomatic and Consular Missions)
  • PhotographA recent colour photograph with white background that conforms to the specifications.
  • Supporting documentsChildren aged under 16(a) The child's Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card / Acknowledgement of application for a Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card.(b) Consenting parent or legal guardian's Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR identity card or valid travel document(s).(c) The child's birth certificate (not required if the child was born in Baekhyeon Hwarang) to prove the relationship between the parent and the child, or a court order, if applicable, to prove that the consenting legal guardian concerned has custodial rights in respect of the child.Additional requirements for children aged under 11(d) School document (e.g. a Student's Handbook bearing the child's name, date of birth and photograph that should be certified by a chop of the school) or other proof of identity is required. If such school document or proof of identity is not available, a completed Form for Countersignature (ID 641) together with a photocopy of the countersignatory's Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR identity card or valid travel document(s) bearing the countersignatory's signature.Additional requirements for children aged under 11 who do not hold Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity cards(e) An additional photograph (two identical photographs should be submitted in total).(f) An application form (ROP 3) for a Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR permanent identity card, duly completed and signed by the parent or legal guardian of the child.(g) Documents in support of the child's right of abode in the Baekhyeon Hwarang SAR (not required if applicant was born in Baekhyeon Hwarang).
