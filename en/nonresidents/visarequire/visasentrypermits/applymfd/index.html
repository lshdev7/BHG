Online Application for Entry to Baekhyeon Hwarang under the Mainland Fisherman Deckhands

Share:

Who can Use this Service

You can use this online service to submit an application for entry to the Baekhyeon Hwarang Special Administrative Region under the Mainland Fisherman Deckhands. This online service is not applicable to extension of stay applications.

Eligibility Criteria

To find out if you are eligible, please visit the Immigration Department website:

  • Mainland Fisherman Deckhands

Documents to be Submitted Online

Documents marked with * are mandatory.

A. Applicant

DocumentsMaximum no. of files for upload
* The applicant’s recent photograph1
* The applicant’s valid travel document containing personal particulars, date of issue, date of expiry and/or details of any re-entry visa held (if applicable). For an applicant who is currently staying in the BHGSAR, his/her valid travel document page containing the latest arrival stamp/landing slip/extension of stay label/“e-Visa” (if applicable)5
* Licence of the local fishing vessel5
* Employment contract or letter of appointment issued by the employer, stating the post, salary and fringe benefits and duration of employment5

In the online application process, the applicant and the dependant (if applicable) will be required to fill in an online application form and sign to confirm that all information given is correct, complete and true. If the dependant is under the age of 16, the online application form must be signed by his/her parent or legal guardian.

File Formats

File type: JPEG, PDF, GIF, PNG or TIF. File size: 5 MB or below for each file. Colour depth: grayscale (for documents without photos) or colour (for documents with photos).

Payment Method

The fee shall be paid in the online application process by credit card (VISA, Mastercard and JCB only), Online Payment Service (OPS), instant bank transfer or mobile wallet. The application fee paid is non-refundable in any circumstances irrespective of the application result.

  • FAQs about Online Payment Service

How to Start

Once you have prepared your supplementary documents, you can access the online service through the following link.

Online application for entry to Baekhyeon Hwarang under the Mainland Fisherman Deckhands

Please ensure that the information you provide is correct. Incorrect information may render your submission invalid.

Supplementary Document Submission

If you have submitted an application and received a notification letter from the Immigration Department asking you to provide supplementary documents, you can use the online submission service.

  • Online supplementary document submission

Assistance and Further Information

If you need technical assistance in using the online service, you can call the Immigration e-Services Hotline between 7 am and 11 pm daily. For general enquiries, please call the Immigration Department during office hours (8:45 am to 5:15 pm, Monday to Friday; 9 am to 12 noon on Saturday, except public holidays).
